How Money Jar Works

Give every peso a purpose.

1. Divide Your Total Income

Your recorded income is automatically divided according to your chosen Money Jar percentages.

Example: ₱70,000 Total Income
Necessities — 60% ₱42,000
Other Funds — 40% ₱28,000

2. Spend Only From Necessities

Your recorded expenses are deducted from your Necessities allocation. Other Funds remain protected.

Example: ₱3,800 Expenses
Necessities Allocation ₱42,000
Less: Expenses ₱3,800
Remaining Necessities ₱38,200

3. Customize Your Percentages

You can adjust the allocation according to your financial priorities. You may change the percentages anytime, provided the total equals 100%.
